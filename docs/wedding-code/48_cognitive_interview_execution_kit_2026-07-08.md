## Purpose

This round is not a launch validation. It is a wording, comprehension, and safety pass.

We are checking whether:

- The 16 questions feel like real wedding-preparation situations.
- Options do not look like moral grades or obvious correct answers.
- The individual result feels like a conversation starter, not a diagnosis.
- The couple-comparison framing feels useful, not like compatibility scoring.
- Any phrase creates pressure, shame, blame, or partner weaponization risk.

Do not claim that WeddingCode is validated after this step. Passing this step only means the next 20-person mini test is worth running.

## Participant Mix

Recruit 5 people with different preparation contexts.

| Slot | Target participant | Why this slot matters |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Just starting wedding conversations or not yet actively booking | Tests whether scenarios are understandable before vendor experience |
| P2 | Currently looking at venues | Tests venue, budget, date, and family-decision realism |
| P3 | Has experienced dress/studio/makeup or quote comparison | Tests vendor and quote-related wording |
| P4 | High family involvement | Tests whether family language avoids blame and shame |
| P5 | Already contracted at least one major item | Tests whether the result still feels relevant after decisions are underway |

Good enough rule: if perfect mixing is not possible, still run 5 interviews. Do not wait for an ideal sample.

## Opening Script

Say this before the participant starts:


## Interview Flow

Total target: 35 to 50 minutes.

| Time | Step | What to do |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-2 min | Consent | Explain purpose, no right answer, anonymous notes |
| 2-7 min | Context | Ask current wedding-preparation stage and biggest active concern |
| 7-32 min | Think-aloud form | Participant answers all 16 questions while speaking reactions aloud |
| 32-40 min | Individual result | Ask what the result seems to be saying and whether it feels fair/useful |
| 40-47 min | Couple comparison | Ask whether they would send it to a partner and what wording changes that feeling |
| 47-50 min | Close | Ask most relatable, most awkward, missing scenario |

## Neutral Probes

Use these instead of leading questions.

- "이 문장을 본 순간 어떤 상황이 떠올랐어요?"
- "고르기 쉬웠나요, 아니면 답이 애매했나요?"
- "선택지 중 정답처럼 보이는 것이 있었나요?"
- "내 답이 없다고 느낀 문항이 있었나요?"
- "이 표현이 나나 상대를 평가하는 느낌이 있었나요?"
- "친구나 partner에게 보낸다면 어떤 말과 같이 보낼 것 같아요?"

Avoid:

- "왜 그렇게 답했어요?" in a challenging tone.
- "이 답변이 더 좋은 답이에요."
- "이 유형이면 이런 사람이에요."
- "궁합", "결혼 성공", "이혼 가능성", "심리 진단".

## Red Flags

Treat these as blockers before a wider mini test.

- Any participant says the product sounds like it predicts relationship success.
- Any participant says the result would be used as a weapon against the partner.
- Two or more participants mark the same item as strong right-answer risk.
- Two or more participants mark the same item as strong stigma/blame risk.
- Two or more participants say no option fits on the same item.
- Three or more participants cannot explain "준비 방식 겹침도" in their own words.
- Three or more participants say they would not share because it feels like evaluation.

## Pass Bar For Moving To 20-Person Mini Test

This is a go/no-go gate, not a proof of validity.

- At least 4 of 5 say the questions mostly feel like real wedding-preparation situations.
- At least 4 of 5 can describe their individual result as a conversation starter.
- At least 3 of 5 say they would send or consider sending the partner link.
- No item has strong right-answer risk from 2 or more participants.
- No item has strong stigma/blame risk from 2 or more participants.
- No severe safety language issue remains unresolved.

## After The 5 Interviews

Create a short synthesis with:

- Top 5 copy fixes.
- Top 3 item/option fixes.
- Whether "준비 방식 겹침도" should remain, be renamed, or be explained differently.
- Whether partner invite should be promoted, softened, or delayed.
- Decision: proceed to 20-person mini test / revise first / stop and redesign.
