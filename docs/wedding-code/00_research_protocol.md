# WeddingCode Research Protocol v0.1

WeddingCode starts from internal, evidence-based constructs, not fixed public letters. The scoring foundation is a set of wedding-planning constructs that can be refined through research, product data, and validation.

Public 4-letter labels and characters are future UI layers. They may help users understand or remember a result later, but they must not define the underlying scoring model.

Characters are an identity and presentation layer, not the scoring foundation.

## Source Grades

- A: official/government sources, peer-reviewed research, official assessment documentation
- B: reputable industry reports and reputable news
- C: marketing case studies and company blogs
- D: social posts, snippets, unverified claims

## Claim Status

- confirmed
- interpretation
- hypothesis
- unknown

## Prohibited Sources And Naming

Do not use MBTI name, questions, type descriptions, or theory structure.

"Wedding MBTI" must not be used as product naming.

Do not copy proprietary questionnaire items, scoring rules, type names, type descriptions, or protected terminology from third-party assessments.

## Safety Boundaries

WeddingCode is not:

- psychological diagnosis
- relationship counseling
- legal advice
- marriage success prediction
- divorce prediction

WeddingCode language should describe wedding-planning preferences, risks, support needs, and collaboration patterns. It must not label a person's worth, mental health, relationship quality, or future marriage outcome.
